🔧 Troubleshooting Guide

Cricut Bluetooth
Not Connecting?

1
Restart everything — Cricut, phone/laptop, and Bluetooth
2
Forget & re-pair — remove your Cricut from Bluetooth settings, pair fresh
3
Check distance — stay within 10-15 ft, no walls between
4
Update firmware — open Design Space, it'll prompt you
